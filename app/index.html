    Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

    PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots). The order below is built from the official descriptions: story chapters first, then the 100% checklist grind, then cleanup. Source: PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; Push Square GTA 6 Trophy Guide.

      100% completion (A Long Road, gold) is the long pole: PowerPyx says it is closer to 60% true completion — most side quests are not required, collectibles only partly. You need 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 of 397 stunts, 15 of 18 card sets and 15 of 18 meditations for the checklist itself.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is flagged as potentially missable by PowerPyx — everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story. Keep chapter saves anyway: there is no mission replay.

        Not missable — but save anyway

        Story trophies (chapters 1–4, the final mission, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) unlock automatically and cannot be missed. Character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) stay available after the story, and quest givers who vanish mid-story return later — PowerPyx.
